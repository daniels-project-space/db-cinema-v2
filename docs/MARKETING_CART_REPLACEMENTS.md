# Marketing-only basket replacements

Browsing and selection remain unchanged. The first review screen (`/cart`) marks marketing-only, inactive, missing, unmapped, calendar-blocked and over-capacity lines unavailable. Checkout stays disabled until availability is resolved, including when arriving directly at `/checkout`.

`convex/lib/marketingInventory.ts` mirrors the explicit model markers in rental-manager `src/data/pricing-catalog.ts` and `src/data/marketing-redirects.ts`, checked against the current RMv2 `items:listActive` and `items:listForReconcile` on 6 October 2026. All 26 excluded inventory identities are covered, including the zero-stock Fuji/Mackie entries. The now-owned DZOFilm Vespid set supersedes its older marketing redirect and stays rentable; none of the current active inventory identities is blocked. These markers must be maintained when the upstream inventory classifications change. Do not substitute the broad Hygglo product flag: it also labels physically owned equipment. Hero item types prevent compatibility references such as “battery for FX6” from blocking real accessories.

`cartReplacements.forCart` returns at most two alternatives per unavailable line. It ranks the existing manager replacement family, mount/focal/class similarity, kit/body shape and daily price. Candidates must have the same item type/category, a valid physical mapping, sufficient stock throughout the exact original dates, and a compatible known mount for retained glass/bodies. Calendar blocks, minimum duration, live/expired holds, confirmed reservations and other basket lines sharing their units are checked. Identical physical configurations are deduplicated. Other unresolved items do not prevent fixing one line at a time. Final checkout still validates the complete kit.

Each card shows its real period price. Switching rechecks suggestions, replaces only the original basket line, preserves dates/position/other lines and the promo, resets recurring-payment consent and refreshes the existing authoritative quotation. A changed basket or now-unavailable candidate produces a retry message. The saved replacement survives reload. The payment hold mutation rejects marketing-only items before creating inventory holds or a Stripe session.

The signed-out account dropdown now anchors below its trigger (`top-full`), matching the signed-in menu. Both dropdowns have a viewport width/height limit and internal scrolling.
