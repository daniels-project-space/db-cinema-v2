## Bounded retention cleanup, 10 October

The existing daily internal cleanup now walks the creation-time index in pages of at most 20 archive records, with requested row/byte read budgets and a scheduled one-second continuation. The first page fixes the creation-time cutoff for the whole sweep. Changing an archive to its deletion tombstone cannot move that cursor, and new arrivals wait for the next sweep. Every archive status is visited, including legacy states and pages containing only tombstones. The old cap of 25 removals per day no longer strands a larger expired backlog.

Each page rechecks the shared actual-return/cancellation retention decision immediately before deletion, including active/reused rentals, open claims and manual holds. Storage failures propagate and preserve the archive status; a later daily sweep can recover. Audit metadata remains after bytes are removed. There is one authoritative daily cron, no new table or index. Daily metadata traversal still includes tombstones, and per-archive indexed document/reuse/case reads remain part of retention evaluation; this change bounds the outer sweep rather than materialising an expiry index.
