fix(mobile hero): stop non-hero videos from eating iOS video decoders

On iOS Safari the homepage hero blanked ~1s after load: the page eagerly
preloaded four 1080p H.264 clips (hero intro + arri-deconstruct + the two
gear/checkout turn overlays), and iOS caps how many videos can hold a hardware
decoder at once, so the hero lost its decode surface and went black. It cannot
repro in headless Chrome/WebKit on Linux because software decode has no cap.

- GearTurnOverlay / CheckoutTurnOverlay: preload="none" and .load() during the
  existing 850/700ms hold beat, so the turn film still buffers in time but no
  longer holds a decoder on the homepage.
- CameraDeconstruct: preload="none" on touch (it already primes on first
  touch); desktop upgrades to "auto" in the effect for scroll-scrub seeking.

Now only the hero holds a decoder at page load, so it plays reliably on mobile.
