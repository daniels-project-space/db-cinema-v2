fix(mobile hero): stop the clip going black on iOS Safari

On iPhone Safari the hero went solid black ~1s in (desktop fine). The only
mobile-only branch was `c1.currentTime = 2` ("start the clip 2s in"), and on
iOS seeking a muted autoplay video while it is still loading blacks it out a
moment later. Removed the seek (mobile now plays from the top), added the
autoplay attribute for reliable iOS muted autoplay, and laid a persistent
cinematic still (intro-poster) under all three clips so a decoder/buffer
hiccup falls back to the still instead of a black box.
